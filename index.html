Selected Projects

Organoid Culture Workcell

Microscope, well-scanning stage, pipetting robot, and robot arm that feed and image living human organoids without the pipette touching them. Sole engineer.

Motorized Focusing Stage

Stepper and lead-screw Z stage for a 10X objective, 0.625 µm per step, with GRBL autofocus. Drift, backlash, and friction fixed with a stronger key and a redesigned casing.

OptoPACE Bioreactor

Turbidostat for phage-assisted continuous evolution, with light setting the selection pressure. Published 2026, co-first author.

Electrochemical Sensor Enclosure

Printed enclosure for an ESP32 potentiostat that reads blood-type antigens by cyclic voltammetry, built for an emergency blood-conversion kit. Led the iGEM hardware team.

Organoid Finite Element Model

Ported a published organoid mechanics model from Abaqus to open-source FEniCS and compared von Mises stress across three maturity stages.

Memristive Synapse

Analog CMOS synapse that stores its weight in a memristor. One circuit produces eight cortical firing patterns.

FailSafe Defect Analysis

Fine-tuned SegFormer that segments porosity in SEM fractographs of printed Ti64, then hands the defect metrics to an LLM for failure diagnosis.

ASL-Translating Glove

ESP32 glove with DIY copper-tape flex sensors that maps handshapes to letters.
