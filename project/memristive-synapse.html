Memristive Synapse for Silicon Neurons

Eight cortical firing patterns from one circuit, with the synaptic weight stored in the memristor itself.

NSF Athena AI Institute, Duke University2026Research assistant

How it works

A CMOS circuit of transistors and capacitors that reproduces cortical spiking: membrane voltage accumulation, threshold and reset, and excitatory or inhibitory synapses. The synaptic weight lives in the memristor itself, so a spiking network can run on the chip with no external memory.

Result

Sweeping memristance from 1 to 100 megaohms drops inter-spike interval from 76.82 ms to 9.39 ms and stretches intraburst time from 2.06 ms to 9.25 ms. One analog weight tunes both spike timing and burst structure.
